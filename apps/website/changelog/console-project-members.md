Project Settings → **Members** lists one row per person and per team with a role on the project: the role, and when it ends, as a date, never, or Expired. A team opens to the people who hold the project through it, and the team that owns the project is listed as admin.

- A row's menu removes its access. Where a person or team holds more than one role, it removes the weakest, and the dialog says when the role they keep ends. A row whose roles have all expired clears every one.
- Your own row has no menu.
- The invitation page shows which account is signed in and offers **Use another account**, so an invitation is not accepted with the wrong one. A link left open in a tab is no longer shown to a different account that signs in there later.

The console's user schema screen lists every revision of the schema, newest first and the latest marked, and opens any of them read-only. An older revision says that users stay on the revision they were created on until they are migrated, and links the migration guide.

The Users list shows the schema revision each user is on, and narrows the list to one schema and to its latest or older revisions, or to one revision, so you can see who is left to migrate.

[Migrate users between schema revisions](https://preview.zitadel.com/docs/guides/migrate-user-schema)

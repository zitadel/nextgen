The console's project settings list everyone with admin access to the project and how each holds it: as a member of the owning team, through a grant to a team they are in, or through a grant of their own. The person who claimed the project appears through the owning team. Removing a grant from someone who also has team access says they keep it.

A release's detail shows a policy revision it pins, in a Policies group with the policy's settings summarised above the document the CLI publishes.

- A user's detail screen counts and dates every passkey they have registered, not only those on the first page.
- The console's sign-in page says what the console is, where you manage your Zitadel projects, and that your app's users sign in through your app, not there.

A project in the console now opens on an **Overview**: its name, site and ID, what its primary origins serve right now, and its latest audit events, each linking to Deployments or Logs.

- The sidebar and top bar stay on Projects and Settings, and the project you selected stays selected. On Projects, the sidebar lists the project's screens (Teams, Users, Logs and the rest).
- A screen that needs a project asks for one with a short picker, then opens.
- Settings has its own list: Profile and, on the hosted cloud, Billing, Support and Email.
- The Projects list reads newest first, and the project settings' first tab is now called General.

These pages explain the model behind the preview and how its pieces fit together.

A [project](/docs/concepts/project-team-user) holds your users and teams, their sessions, and your configuration. That configuration lives in your repository as files ([Configuration as code](/docs/concepts/repo-config)). The [user schemas and login flows](/docs/concepts/flows-and-schemas) decide what a user is and how they sign in. A sign-in runs as an [authentication attempt](/docs/concepts/sessions-auth-attempts) and ends in a session. The server keeps all of it in one of the [databases](/docs/concepts/databases) it supports.

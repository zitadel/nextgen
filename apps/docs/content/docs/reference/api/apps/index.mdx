---
title: Apps
description: OpenAPI operations for apps, their clients' credentials, and the clients that registered themselves.
---

An app is one piece of software that uses Zitadel, and one document holds its
clients, the APIs it serves and whether clients may register into it. The
developer's own apps are declared in the release; an app a product creates for
its customers is registered here, by its slug. A client's credentials, named
secrets and keys, are written on their own and outlive every deploy. Clients
that registered themselves join the app whose API they first asked for, and
are listed and deleted here.

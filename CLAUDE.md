## Agent Handoff

Keep `AGENT_HANDOFF.md` continuously up to date throughout this session.

**Run `/handoff` automatically:**
- After completing each meaningful chunk of work (feature, fix, refactor)
- After completing an item of the TODOs list
- Before any risky operation (large refactor, migration, delete)
- When asked to stop or hand off
- Whenever context is getting long

The mechanical git state (branch, commits, diff) is updated automatically by hooks on every stop and compaction. Your job is to keep the "Notes from Claude" section current so another agent can continue without losing context.

Once you've filled up the "Notes from Claude" section, check that the update has been done successfully.

Do not wait to be asked.

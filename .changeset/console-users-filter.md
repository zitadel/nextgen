The console's Users list has one **Add filter** menu in place of its schema and revision selects: it lists what the users can be narrowed by, then the values of the property you choose. Besides a schema and its revisions, the list can now be narrowed by status and by when a user was created.

A status's dot now says what the status means, on every list and detail page: green for one in force, amber for a suspended user, red for one pending purge, grey for one that is off.

The Users and Teams lists' buttons say what they add, **Add user** and **Add team**, and a sidebar row that opens a list of its own (Logs, Settings) carries an arrow.

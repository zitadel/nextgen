The console's Events screen reads more like a log. Its filters are groups that fold away, and the categories, a request's status classes and how a sign-in ended are checkboxes, several at once. Each row leads with its time, as a day and a clock time to the millisecond, and the API requests view shows a request's method and operation, status and duration in columns of their own. An open event no longer covers the list: on a wide window the list ends where the event's drawer starts, so its search, Live, Refresh and Export stay in reach, and the suggestions under the search no longer open over the drawer. The drawer's sections are ruled off like the console's other drawers. A list that a user, operation or status filter leaves empty says that events recorded before the server had that filter are not matched.

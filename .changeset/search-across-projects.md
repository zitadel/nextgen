`POST /search` on a console session without `project_id` now searches the newest 10 projects the person can act on ([Search](https://preview.zitadel.com/docs/reference/api/search)), and the console's search (⌘K) does so from All projects, Projects and Settings. Teams and configuration now match from the start of a name or of a word in it, no longer inside a word.

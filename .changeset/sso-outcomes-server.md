A flow now resolves the identity a sign-in provider returned. When the provider's account is already linked to a user, `GET /flow/{id}` signs that user in: the response carries the terminal step and the handoff token. When the connection sets `provisioning.creation` to `disabled` and the account has no user, the step is shown again with `error.sso_creation_disabled`. A provider account that belongs to a user the flow cannot continue with ends in `409` with `flow.restart_required`. `GET /flow/{id}` now renews the flow cookie whenever it answers with a step, clears it when the flow completes, and sets none on error responses, matching submit. Sessions list a new `sso` factor method. The SSO transition outcomes are renamed: `callback` is now `sso_authenticated` (required on a step that offers `sso_providers`) and `identity_unknown` is now `sso_user_not_found`. Rename these keys in your flow definitions. `user_already_exists` keeps its name and is shared with the typed collision.

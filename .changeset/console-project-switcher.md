The console's project switcher is a searchable list: it opens on a search field, so typing a name and pressing Enter switches project. It opens on the project you are in and the ones you opened recently, then, while all your projects fit on one page, every other one by name, and ends with All projects. Past one page it keeps to the recent ones and finds the rest on the server, so the list stays short for anyone who can act on many projects, such as a platform operator. Each project carries a generated mark and, under its name, its site's host or its id, so two projects of the same name can be told apart.

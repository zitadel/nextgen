The password step of `<zitadel-login>` shows the account as a full-width chip that goes back to use another account, with an avatar generated from the typed identifier, so it never confirms an account exists; the console shows the same avatar. Custom templates get the chip as `<zl-account>`, and `<zl-avatar>` and `@zitadel/components/avatar` export the avatar for your own pages.

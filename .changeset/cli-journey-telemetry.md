The CLI reports how developers move through its journeys: the setup wizard and OIDC provider setup as flows with their steps and outcomes, and every command's outcome with the facts it knows, such as the sign-in method, the identity provider kind, the access level a grant gives, and the stage and configuration a deploy changed. Nothing is sent without the existing telemetry consent, and no entered value is ever included.
